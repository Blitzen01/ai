74 Days 💛

since we first met

74 days since we first met… and honestly, ang dami nang nangyari in such a short time. Pero kahit ganun, every moment with you feels real and special. Hindi lang basta araw yung lumipas—each one became a memory that I’ll always keep, especially because it’s with you.

Sa loob ng 74 days na ‘to, mas nakilala kita, and mas lalo kitang na-appreciate. Yung mga simpleng usapan natin, yung tawanan, kahit yung mga tampuhan—lahat yun naging parte ng kung ano tayo ngayon. And kahit hindi perfect, I wouldn’t trade what we have for anything else.

May mga times na nahihirapan tayo, na parang ang gulo na ng lahat. Pero kahit ganun, ikaw pa rin yung gusto kong balikan. Ikaw pa rin yung pipiliin ko araw-araw, kahit may mga araw na hindi madali. Kasi para sakin, mas importante na nandito tayo, trying, kaysa sumuko.

Hindi ko alam kung ano mangyayari sa future, pero ang alam ko ngayon, mahalaga ka sakin. And I want to keep building more days, more memories, more reasons para mas maging strong pa tayo. Hindi lang 74 days—gusto ko pa ng mas madami, kasama ka.

So thank you for these 74 days… for staying, for trying, and for being you. I may not always say it perfectly, pero I truly care about you. And if papipiliin ako ulit, I’d still choose you—over and over again. 💛
